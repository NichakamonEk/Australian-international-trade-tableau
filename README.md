# Australian International Trade (ABS 1988–2024) — Tableau Visual Analytics

▪️ Data visualisation project analysing Australia’s export/import trends from 1988 to 2024 using ABS data  
▪️ Focused on category structure, trade balance, key partners, and a deep-dive on Crude Materials (SITC 2)  
▪️ Delivered as an interactive dashboard + storyboard with forecasting and strategic recommendations  

---

## ▸ Project Overview
This project investigates Australia’s international trade performance from 1988–2024 using ABS data across 10 main categories and 67 subcategories (SITC). The analysis highlights long-term structural shifts, major global event impacts, and a focused drill-down into Metalliferous Ores & Scrap (SITC 28) and Iron Ore.

---

## ▸ Business Objective
▪️ Explain how Australia’s export/import structure has evolved over time  
▪️ Identify major trade partners and concentration risk  
▪️ Compare trends by category (AUD value, %YoY change, and %share)  
▪️ Deep-dive Crude Materials (SITC 2) to identify key export drivers and future outlook  

---

## ▸ Analytical Scope
▪️ National trade overview (export, import, and trade balance)  
▪️ Trade partner concentration (export vs import)  
▪️ Category-level trends and volatility across time  
▪️ Crude Materials deep-dive with a subcategory and product focus (SITC 28 / Iron Ore)  
▪️ Forecasting (exponential smoothing) and recommendations  

---

## ▸ Key Insights (Summary)
▪️ Long-term shift from frequent deficits to sustained trade surplus post-2010  
▪️ Export growth is structurally concentrated in resource-based categories  
▪️ Import structure is dominated by high-value manufactured/technology categories  
▪️ Crude Materials (SITC 2) is a major export pillar, driven by Metalliferous Ores (SITC 28)  
▪️ Iron Ore is the primary driver within SITC 28 and supports a strong forward outlook under uncertainty  

---

## ▸ Deliverables
▪️ Tableau dashboard: Subcategory focus — Metalliferous Ores & Scrap (SITC 28)  
▪️ Tableau storyboard: Crude Materials narrative with event context and turning points
